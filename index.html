Questions people ask

Does it work with WhatsApp?

Yes. Connect the WhatsApp Business Cloud API and incoming messages create or update leads. Your team replies from a shared inbox and every message stays on the lead’s record.

Can I run a separate pipeline for each client?

Yes. Each workspace has its own stages, fields, layouts and branding, so every client can follow their own process.

Can I control what each person sees?

Roles set read, create, edit and delete rights per record type, can limit a person to their own records, and can hide or lock single fields. Admins can preview the app as any role before giving it out.

Can I bring in my existing leads?

Yes. Import a CSV and map its columns to your fields, or send leads from other tools to the ingest endpoint.

Is there an audit trail?

Every record change, sign-in, failed sign-in and permission change is logged with who did it and when.
