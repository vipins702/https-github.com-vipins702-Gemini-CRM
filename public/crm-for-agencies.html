CRM for agencies: questions

Can each client have a different pipeline?

Yes. Every workspace has its own stages, required fields and stage actions, so a lead-gen client and a retainer client can follow different steps.

Can clients see their own leads?

You can give a client a login with a read-only role limited to their workspace, so they see their leads and nothing else.

Does it capture Instagram and Facebook leads?

Yes. Leads sent to the ingest endpoint from Instagram, Facebook or any form tool are created as leads with the source recorded, and duplicates are matched by e-mail.
