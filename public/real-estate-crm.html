How FlowCRM helps

Enquiries become leads automatically

A first WhatsApp message creates a lead with the phone number already matched, so no enquiry sits unanswered in someone’s personal phone.

Site visits never slip

Moving a buyer to “Site visit booked” can create the follow-up task for the right agent, and the calendar shows every visit for the day.

Brokers see only their buyers

Give each broker a role limited to their own leads, while managers see the whole pipeline and the source of every booking.

real estate CRM: questions

Can I import leads from property portals?

Yes. Import a CSV export, or post leads from any portal or form tool to the ingest endpoint so they arrive as they come in.

Can I track which project a buyer is interested in?

Add your own fields, such as project, budget and configuration, to the lead form without writing code.
