How FlowCRM helps

Every enquiry gets a counsellor

Website and social enquiries are shared out by round-robin or by rules, such as course or city, so nobody waits for a callback.

Follow-ups happen on time

Drip e-mail sequences and stage tasks remind students and counsellors about deadlines, demo classes and pending documents.

See what fills batches

Reports show enquiries by source, course and counsellor, so you know which campaigns bring students who actually enrol.

CRM for education: questions

Can parents and students be on one record?

Yes. Add custom fields for the parent’s name and contact, or link a separate contact record.

Can we run different steps for different courses?

Yes. Make a workspace per course or branch, each with its own pipeline and required fields.
